A song written just for you

Bruce, age 10, writes and performs custom songs for birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, and any other special occasion. Tell him about the person and the occasion, and he will turn your story into a one-of-a-kind song, delivered as an audio recording or a video of Bruce performing it.

Songs start at $20

How it works

  1. Contact us with the occasion, who the song is for, and a few details to include (names, favourite things, funny stories).
  2. Let us know whether you would like audio or video, and when you need it.
  3. Bruce writes and records your song, and we send it to you to share.
